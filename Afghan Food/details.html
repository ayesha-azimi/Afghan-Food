Kabuli Pulao

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 1 lb lamb or beef
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 1/2 cup raisins
  • 1/2 cup shredded carrots
  • Spices (cinnamon, cardamom, cumin, salt)

Instructions:

  1. Boil rice until half cooked. Drain and set aside.
  2. Fry onion and meat until browned.
  3. Add water and cook meat until tender.
  4. Fry carrots and raisins separately with a bit of sugar.
  5. Layer meat and rice, top with carrot and raisin mix.
  6. Steam everything together for 20 minutes.
